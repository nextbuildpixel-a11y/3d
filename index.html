Crispness & Reheating Guide
Restore waffle perfection in minutes
▼
🥤 Air Fryer (Best)
Set to 180°C for 2 minutes. Place waffle flat on the basket. Flip once at 1 min. Result: golden-crisp exterior restored perfectly.
⏰ Oven Method
Preheat to 200°C. Place on wire rack for 4-5 minutes. This preserves the internal moisture while re-crisping the outer surface.
💨 Toaster Trick
Use the lowest heat setting on a standard toaster for 1 cycle. Works best for plain waffles without toppings already applied.
❗ Avoid Microwave
Microwave makes waffles soggy. Always use dry heat methods to restore the original crispy Belgian texture.
